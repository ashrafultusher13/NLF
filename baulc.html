About BAULC

Bangldesh Agricultural University Language Club

The Bangladesh Agricultural University Language Club (BAULC) is a vibrant and dynamic organization dedicated to promoting language learning, cultural exchange, and effective communication skills among students. Established with the vision of fostering a love for languages and enhancing linguistic proficiency, BAULC serves as a platform for students to explore diverse languages, engage in interactive language activities, and participate in cultural events.
