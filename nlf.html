About NLF

National Language Feast 2026

The National Language Feast (NLF) is a prestigious event organized by the BAU Language Club (BAULC) at Bangladesh Agricultural University. It serves as a platform to celebrate linguistic diversity, promote cultural exchange, and foster a deeper understanding of languages and their significance in society. NLF brings together language enthusiasts, scholars, and students from various backgrounds to engage in discussions, workshops, and activities that highlight the importance of preserving and promoting languages in an increasingly globalized world.

History & Evolution

NLF History & Evolution

NLF 23

The inaugural NLF event was held in 2023, marking the beginning of a journey to celebrate languages and cultures. It featured a variety of activities, including language workshops, cultural performances, and interactive sessions that engaged participants in meaningful discussions about linguistic diversity.

NLF 24

Building on the success of the first event, NLF 2024 expanded its reach and introduced new segments, attracting a larger audience and fostering collaborations with language experts and cultural organizations. The event emphasized the role of languages in promoting social cohesion and understanding.

NLF 25

NLF 2025 continued to grow in popularity, featuring a diverse range of activities that highlighted the importance of language preservation and revitalization. The event showcased innovative approaches to language learning and encouraged participants to explore the rich tapestry of global languages and cultures.
